About me

Hi, I'm Estevo. I hold a degree in Computer Engineering from the Universidade da Coruña and I'm currently studying the International Master in Computer Vision (IMCV), taught entirely in English. I try to learn something new every day about anything related to Computer Science.

During my degree, the fields that caught my attention the most were Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Computer Vision (CV). That led me to choose the Computing specialization and, later on, to work for 9 months in the Computer Vision department of the Instituto Tecnológico de Galicia (ITG). I'm also interested in Motion Capture and its applications.

Personal manifesto

I believe technological development (especially in AI) should always serve the general good of society, helping to build a better world for everyone. Even if the impact is small, or even unnoticeable to the user, I also aim to improve the interaction between humans and machines so that it is accessible to everyone.

How I work

  • I research the technologies involved and the goals before starting.
  • I take notes in Obsidian on every requirement.
  • I work steadily, without procrastinating, trying to understand every step I take.
  • I present results while internalizing and summarizing what I learned so I can reuse it in the future.
